Privacy Policy for HearPod

Package Name: com.aistudio.hearpod.qxkpv
Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Contact Email: brightquestofficial@gmail.com

HearPod ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how HearPod handles audio data, device permissions, subscriptions, and analytics when you use the HearPod Android application.

1. Microphone & Audio Processing (On-Device)

HearPod requests the Android RECORD_AUDIO permission solely to capture live microphone input and deliver real-time hearing amplification, noise reduction, and equalizer filtering to your connected earbuds or speaker.

  • Live Audio Stays On Your Device: Real-time microphone audio is processed strictly in memory on your device and is never streamed, uploaded, or shared with third-party servers.
  • Optional Local Recordings: If you tap "Record" to save a hearing session, the audio file (.wav) is stored privately inside your device's internal application storage. You can replay or permanently delete any recording inside the app at any time.

2. Bluetooth & Audio Routing Permissions

HearPod uses BLUETOOTH and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT permissions exclusively to detect connected wireless earbuds/headsets, display headphone battery status, and route low-latency amplified audio to your selected listening device. We do not track Bluetooth device identifiers or scan for nearby third-party devices for advertising or location profiling.

3. Optional AI Acoustic Optimization

When you tap AI Optimize, HearPod may send anonymous numeric acoustic telemetry (ambient decibel level, peak decibel level, and connected output device type) to the Google Gemini API solely to recommend equalizer settings. No raw voice audio, microphone recordings, or personal identifiers are ever transmitted. If offline or unconfigured, optimization runs locally on your device.

4. Subscriptions & Payments (Google Play Billing)

HearPod offers optional paid subscriptions (hearpod_pro_monthly and hearpod_pro_annual) processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, access, or store your credit card numbers, billing address, or financial details. Google Play handles all billing transactions and subscription management under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Foreground Service & Notifications

HearPod uses a foreground microphone service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE) and POST_NOTIFICATIONS permission so live amplification continues reliably when your screen locks and provides a persistent notification control to stop amplification at any time.

6. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

All hearing thresholds, equalizer presets, saved audio sessions, and preferences are stored locally on your Android device. You can delete individual recorded sessions directly in the app's Saved Sessions sheet, clear app data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all local data immediately.

7. Children's Privacy

HearPod is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us
