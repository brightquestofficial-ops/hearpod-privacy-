Privacy Policy for HearPod Effective Date: November 13, 2026 Package: com.aistudio.hearpod.qxkpv Contact: brightquestofficial@gmail.com 1. Microphone & Audio Processing HearPod requests RECORD_AUDIO solely to capture live microphone input and provide real-time hearing amplification, noise reduction, and equalizer filtering on your device. Live microphone audio is processed strictly in memory on your device and is never streamed or uploaded to external servers. If you choose to record a session, the audio file (.wav) is stored locally on your device and can be deleted inside the app at any time. 2. Bluetooth Permissions BLUETOOTH and BLUETOOTH_CONNECT are used solely to detect connected earbuds/headsets, show headphone battery info, and route amplified audio to your listening device. 3. AI Acoustic Optimization When you tap AI Optimize, only anonymous numeric acoustic levels (ambient dB, peak dB, and output device type) are sent to generate equalizer recommendations. No voice recordings or personal identifiers are transmitted. 4. Subscriptions & Payments Subscriptions (hearpod_pro_monthly and hearpod_pro_annual) are processed securely via Google Play Billing. HearPod does not collect or store credit card or billing details. 5. Notifications & Foreground Service POST_NOTIFICATIONS and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE are used to keep live amplification running reliably when your screen locks and display a persistent control to stop amplification anytime. 6. Data Deletion & Contact All settings, audiogram thresholds, and recorded sessions are stored locally on your device and are removed when deleted in-app or upon uninstalling HearPod. Contact brightquestofficial@gmail.com with any privacy questions.
